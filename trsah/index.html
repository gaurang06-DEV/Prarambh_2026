Frames of Time

Rules & Guidelines

  • Each team must submit 2 photographs with a short caption
  • First photograph must depict archival Goa (forts, heritage sites, monuments)
  • Second photograph must reflect modern or developing Goa
  • Accepted formats: JPEG / JPG / PNG (Minimum resolution: 1080p)
  • Basic editing allowed (brightness, contrast, crop). No heavy manipulation
  • Submission deadline: 5th March 2026, before 12:00 Noon
  • AI-generated images are strictly prohibited

About Prarambh 2026

Prarambh is the annual cultural fest of our college, bringing together students from various disciplines to celebrate art, culture, and talent. Join us for a series of exciting events, performances, and competitions that showcase the vibrant spirit of our community. Experience the rich heritage of Goa through creative expressions, dynamic performances, and unforgettable moments.
