About Prarambh 2026

Prarambh is the annual cultural fest of our college, bringing together students from various disciplines to celebrate art, culture, and talent. Join us for a series of exciting events, performances, and competitions that showcase the vibrant spirit of our community. Experience the rich heritage of Goa through creative expressions, dynamic performances, and unforgettable moments.
